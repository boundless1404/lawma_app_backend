About WastePro

WastePro is a comprehensive waste management platform developed by BoundlessEdge to streamline waste collection, billing, and management operations across Lagos State and beyond.

Built with modern technologies including NestJS backend and Quasar Framework frontend, WastePro offers a robust, scalable solution for waste management organizations of all sizes.

The platform integrates seamlessly with existing operations while providing advanced features like automated billing, comprehensive reporting, and multi-role user management.
